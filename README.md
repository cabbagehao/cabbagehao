## Photo to Coloring Page [image to coloring](https://gencoloring.ai)

This feature converts photos into coloring pages. Just upload an image and the AI identifies outlines and transforms them into line drawings. I've tried uploading family and pet photos - the results are pretty good with clear line extraction.

This feature can be used to:
- Turn family photos into coloring materials for kids
- Create personalized coloring pages from pet photos
- Convert travel photos into artistic creation materials
- Make unique birthday gifts

After processing, you can download in PDF format ready for printing.
